Nearly half of startup deals collapse during technical due diligence

Due diligence typically costs 0.2–4% of deal value ($25K–$75K at seed, $50K–$200K+ at Series A/acquisition). Reviewers report they can tell when documentation was assembled in a rush specifically for the diligence process — it reads as inconsistent, polished in some areas and thin in others — versus documentation built continuously as part of normal engineering practice, which reads as a signal of engineering discipline.
