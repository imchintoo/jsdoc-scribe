3. Signal worth writing about today

Security questionnaires now carry AI/data-handling questions no tool docs page answers directly

The 2026 refresh of both major vendor-risk frameworks (CAIQ v4, SIG/SIG Lite) adds expanded AI and data-handling content on top of the existing 17–18 security domains. For a small vendor or an internal tool being evaluated by a procurement or security team, these questionnaires are usually answered from scratch each time — there's rarely a pre-written, tool-specific answer key sitting anywhere, even though the underlying facts (does data leave the environment, is there a third-party API call, what's the license, who are the subprocessors) don't change questionnaire to questionnaire. This is the open backlog item from the 2026-08-06 report: rather than another explainer about why due diligence matters (already covered 2026-07-23, 2026-07-29), this is the first post that functions as a literal reference — mapping specific, real CAIQ/SIG question categories to specific, factual jsdoc-scribe answers, in a format Chintan or a teammate could paste straight into an actual questionnaire response.
